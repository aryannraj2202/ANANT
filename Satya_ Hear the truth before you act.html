Four steps, all on your phone

Your audio is never uploaded. Satya checks it locally and keeps only the result.

  1. ListenPick a voice note, or turn on call monitoring. Audio is cleaned and cut into short windows.
  2. AnalyzeSmall on-device models look at voice texture, rhythm, background noise, and video frames.
  3. CombineNo single check decides. The evidence is merged into one score from 0 to 100.
  4. AdviseYou get a colour, a short reason, and a safe next step, such as calling back on a known number.

A result you understand in seconds

Three states, not a pile of percentages. The middle state exists on purpose, so Satya never pretends to be sure when it isn’t.

Low riskScore 85 to 100

Human-like voice. Carry on, and stay aware.

UncertainScore 50 to 84

Mixed evidence. If the request is sensitive, confirm who it is first.

High riskScore below 50

Several signs of a synthetic voice. Do not send money or share an OTP.

Why Satya is different

Other tools exist. Satya is built around three promises.

Private by design

Analysis runs on the device. Your family’s voice notes are not the product.

Warns during the call

The score updates every second or two, so you hear the warning while the caller is still talking.

Explains itself

Tap Why? to see the signals behind a result. A warning, never an accusation.
